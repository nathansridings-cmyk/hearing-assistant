Doc Scanner Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026. Doc Scanner is made by Shane Ridings.

What stays on your phone

Page photos, thumbnails, and PDFs are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. Finding the page edges, cropping, straightening, filters, and optional text recognition all run on the phone. Nothing you scan is uploaded.

Doc Scanner does not create an account. It does not include advertising or analytics. It does not track you.

Camera

The camera is used only to photograph a page so the scanner can crop and straighten it. Doc Scanner does not record video and does not watch the camera in the background.

If you choose an existing photo, only the pictures you pick are imported. The app does not browse your photo library in the background.

When something leaves the phone

If you tap Share, the PDF is handed to the app you choose, such as Mail, Messages, or Drive. That app’s privacy policy then applies.

Optional cards in the free version open the Google Play or App Store page for another app. The store, not Doc Scanner, receives that visit.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase. Payment is handled by Google Play on Android and by Apple on iPhone and iPad. Doc Scanner does not receive your card number, and it does not send purchase details to a server of ours.

Children

Doc Scanner is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to Shane Ridings through the contact listed on the store page for Doc Scanner.
