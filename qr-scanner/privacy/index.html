Privacy policy for QR Scanner

Last updated: October 10, 2026

QR Scanner is made by Nathan Shane Ridings. You can write to simpleassistapps@gmail.com. This policy says what the app does with information. It is meant to be plain and direct.

The short version

QR Scanner does not collect, sell, or share personal information. It does not show pop-ups or third-party ads. It does not include analytics or tracking tools. It does not have an account. It does not use an internet connection of its own.

Camera

The camera is used only after you choose Scan with camera and allow Camera. The app reads codes in the image on your phone, with a barcode model that is part of the app and works offline. Camera images are not saved, not uploaded, and not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Photos

If you choose Scan a saved photo, you pick a picture with the system photo picker. The app reads that picture on your phone to look for a code. The picture is not uploaded. The app does not ask for access to your photo library.

Scan history

The text inside codes you scan is saved on your phone so you can open it later. That can include a link, a phone number, a message, or a Wi-Fi password. This history stays on the phone. It is not sent to the developer. It is not included in Android cloud backup or in device-to-device transfer. You can delete one scan, or clear the whole history, at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes it too.

Links and sharing

If you choose Open link, Copy, Share, Call, Email, or a similar button, your phone hands that information to the app you pick, such as your browser or your dialer. That other app has its own privacy policy. Before a web link opens, QR Scanner shows you the full address and asks you to confirm.

Hearing Assistant

The home screen, and the history screen, may show a small card for another app by the same developer, Hearing Assistant. Google Play treats that card as an ad because it promotes the developer's own app. It is not a third-party ad, it is not a pop-up, and it does not track you. If you tap it, your phone opens the Google Play listing for Hearing Assistant, or hearingassistant.app. You can dismiss the card.

Permissions

The only permission the app requests is Camera. Scanning a saved photo uses the system picker and does not request storage or photo access.

Children

QR Scanner is a general utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the privacy policy address listed on Google Play, and inside the app. The date at the top will change.
