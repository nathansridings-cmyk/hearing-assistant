Helping People Hear More

A little help with everyday sounds.

Hearing Assistant is designed for older adults and anyone who wants nearby conversations, television, or other everyday sounds to be easier to hear. The app keeps the choices clear and the controls large.

Good to know: Hearing Assistant is a sound-amplification app. It does not diagnose or treat hearing loss and is not a replacement for a medical hearing aid or professional care.

Tester & Help

Trying Hearing Assistant?

Connect your earbuds or headphones, choose a microphone, start at a low amplification level, and turn Hearing ON. Increase the level gradually to a comfortable setting.

Reduce feedback

Put your earbuds in before turning Hearing ON, and keep the phone away from the earbuds.

Share feedback

Testers can report what worked, what was confusing, and which phone and earbuds they used.
