Privacy policy for Hearing Assistant

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Hearing Assistant is made by Nathan Shane Ridings. You can write to simpleassistapps@gmail.com. This policy applies to the Hearing Assistant app on Android and on iPhone. It says what the app does with information. It is meant to be plain and direct.

The short version

Hearing Assistant collects no personal data. Audio is processed in memory on the device and is never saved or uploaded. If you use the optional voice setup, the app stores a small numeric voice profile on your device only; you can delete it anytime with DELETE MY VOICE PROFILE. App settings (like volume boost level) are saved on the device only. There are no accounts. There is no tracking or analytics. There are no third-party ads.

Audio

The app uses the microphone to amplify nearby sounds through your earbuds or headphones. Audio is processed in memory on the device and is never saved or uploaded. If you use the optional voice setup, the app stores a small numeric voice profile on your device only; you can delete it anytime with DELETE MY VOICE PROFILE. App settings (like volume boost level) are saved on the device only.

Accounts, tracking, and ads

Hearing Assistant does not ask you to create an account. It does not include tracking or analytics. It does not show third-party ads.

Android and iPhone

The same rules apply on Android and on iPhone. On both, the app collects no personal data, and audio stays on the device.

Children

Hearing Assistant is a general sound-amplification app. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address. The effective date at the top will change.
