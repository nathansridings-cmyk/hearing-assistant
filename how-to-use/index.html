Hearing Assistant

How to Use

Follow these five steps in order. Bold words are the words you will see on the app screen.

Steps 1 and 2 make nearby sounds louder. Steps 3, 4, and 5 are for talking with another person.

Good to know: Hearing Assistant is a sound-amplification app. It does not diagnose or treat hearing loss and is not a replacement for a medical hearing aid or professional care.

  1. 1

    Turn it on to amplify sounds

    Put in your earbuds or headphones. Wired or Bluetooth both work.

    Then tap START HEARING.

    Tap STOP HEARING to stop.

  2. 2

    Adjust the volume

    Use the VOLUME BOOST slider.

    It goes from 1× to 10×.

    Start low. Turn it up slowly until the sound is comfortable.

  3. 3

    Record your voice for Conversation Mode

    In VOICE SETUP FOR CONVERSATION MODE, tap RECORD MY VOICE.

    Speak normally in a quiet place for about 6 seconds.

    It stops on its own and shows YOUR VOICE IS READY.

    You can redo it with RECORD MY VOICE AGAIN.

    Hearing turns off for this short recording.

  4. 4

    Set the background sound

    Set the BACKGROUND SOUND slider low, toward QUIET.

    QUIET is the left end.

  5. 5

    Turn on Conversation Mode

    If hearing is off, tap START HEARING.

    Then tap TURN CONVERSATION MODE ON.

    It recognizes your own voice and turns it down in your earbuds.

    Other people's voices still come through.

    This is voice recognition. It is not captions or speech-to-text.

    For TV, tap TURN CONVERSATION MODE OFF.

Good to know: Hearing Assistant is a sound-amplification app. It does not diagnose or treat hearing loss and is not a replacement for a medical hearing aid or professional care.
